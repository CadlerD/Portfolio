About Me

I'm Cadler D'ahiti, currently pursuing Data Science and Computer Science at UWM, I strive utilize technology to create a real impact in my community. Beyond the scope of computers, I've always been curious about how systems worked with people and what they could give to an environment physically and socially. This curiousity grew more into a passion for technology and social work, leading me to double major in Data and Computer Science, so I could see both lens using technology. Before college I've built foundations in programming, statistics and data visualization, while also educating myself in the social world by taking leadership roles and committing to all sorts of communities. That behavior didn't stop with highschool, in my first year going to UWM, I committing myself to being the Programs Chair at the Nation Society of Black Engineers(NSBE) UWM Chapter and establish myself at the Student Association at UWM. One of my long term goals is to research AI ethics and management, because as artificial intelligence makes a rise in the coming decade, I fully believe that we have a responsibility to handle the field with care so it's used fairly and transparently. Being a part of a movement that ensures proper regulation of AI in human hands and a developed system guided by policies to safeguard our future is one of my biggest desires. I'll always be on the lookout for opportunities to grow, contribute and use data to make a impact on something or someone.
